# Pre-registration: transfer and explanation experiments

Written 2026-09-26, on top of commit `5b613cb`, **before** any outcome for the new target models
below was loaded or analyzed. Only prompt coverage and per-prompt cost were read for them (to
confirm every candidate answers all 4,934 LLMRouterBench prompts). Methods, splits, metrics and
endpoints are frozen here; any later deviation is reported as a deviation.

## Frozen components

- Questions: v1, fingerprint `4f6b3c061523` (`src/dmroute/questions.py`). The 13 feature questions
  never mention the model pool, so the cached Jev (`jev-1.13.0`) answers are reused unchanged.
- Router-visible text: as in the original test configs (Jev: `max_chars` 16,000; embeddings: 2,000).
- Heads: per-model logistic regression, C = 1 (`_PerModelLogistic`), except `bgeL_logreg`, whose C is
  chosen by stratified CV on the labeled examples only (`_TunedPerModelLogistic`).
- Split: LLMRouterBench seed-42 test split with design-seen prompts moved to train (1,328 test
  prompts); label seeds 42, 999, 2024, 2025, 3407; k in {25, 50, 100, 200, all}.
- Metric: within-category AUROC of P(target model answers correctly), n-weighted over categories
  with at least 10 of each outcome in the test split (`metrics.calibration.by_category`).

## Experiment T: success prediction for unseen models

The routing label "the cheap model suffices" depends only on the cheap model, so a new model pair is
a new target model. Targets: every model in the LLMRouterBench release that answers all 4,934
prompts, excluding the three models in the original pool (deepseek-v3.1-terminus, deepseek-r1-0528,
gpt-5, which shaped question design) and `openrouter` (not a single named model). That gives
**9 targets**: qwen3-235b-a22b-2507, deepseek-v3-0324, kimi-k2-0905, intern-s1, gemini-2.5-flash,
gpt-5-chat, glm-4.6, claude-sonnet-4, gemini-2.5-pro. Label: record score >= 0.5.

Methods (no changes after seeing target results): `jev:features`; baselines `tfidf_logreg`,
`knn10`, `bge_logreg`, `bge_knn`, `bgeL_logreg` (CV-tuned C), `bgeL_knn`.

- **Primary endpoint.** At k = 25: mean over the 9 targets of (jev:features minus the primary
  baseline) in within-category AUROC. 95% CI by hierarchical bootstrap resampling targets, label
  seeds and test prompts (stratified by category). **Success: CI lower bound > 0.**
- **Primary baseline**, fixed by rule before any target result exists: the text router with the
  highest mean within-category AUROC at k = 25 on the *original* target (deepseek-v3.1-terminus) in
  the rerun that includes `bgeL`; ties broken by the order listed above.
- Secondary: per-target differences; every k; every baseline; labels-to-target (the smallest k at
  which a method's mean AUROC reaches jev:features' k = 25 mean on that target, "not reached" if
  none); label cost = k x the target model's mean per-prompt cost from the release.

## Experiment D: few-shot adaptation to a held-out domain

Original target (deepseek-v3.1-terminus). Held-out domains with >= 100 test prompts:
livecodebench, mmlupro, swe-bench. Heads trained on all other domains' train-split labels plus
k in {0, 25, 50, 100} labels sampled from the held-out domain's train split (5 seeds); scored on the
held-out domain's test split. Methods: jev:features, the primary baseline, tfidf_logreg.
Primary: at k = 25, mean over the 3 domains of (jev:features minus primary baseline), bootstrap CI
over seeds and test prompts; success if lower bound > 0.

## Experiment E: why does it work? (original target, same splits and seeds)

1. **Same questions, different answerer:** `openai/gpt-6-luna` answers the 13 questions (verbalized
   probabilities, one structured call per prompt, 2,000-char text), same head; k <= 200 (already
   running as `scripts/llm_features_ablation.py`, started before this note).
2. **Fewer dimensions:** bge-base and bge-large embeddings reduced to 13 dimensions by PCA fitted on
   the unlabeled train prompts, same C = 1 head.
3. **Question count:** random subsets of 1, 3 and 6 of the 13 Jev features (20 draws each, numpy
   seed 0) and all 13, same head; report the mean over draws. No best-subset selection.
